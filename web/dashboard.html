Firmware update
Upload .pio/build/esp32dev/firmware.bin. The board writes it to the spare app slot and only switches over once the whole image has arrived, so a failed upload leaves the running firmware untouched.
From a shell: curl -F firmware=@.pio/build/esp32dev/firmware.bin http://lucas.local/update
